The Sower

Situation

The Sower (Auspicious). You scatter seeds across a field, knowing that each one has the potential to grow into a bountiful harvest.

Guidance

Invest time and effort into building relationships, developing skills, and exploring new ideas. These investments will pay off in the future, often in unexpected ways.

Outlook

The seeds you plant today will yield rich rewards tomorrow, though you may not see the results immediately.
